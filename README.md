### A Smarter Way to Move Value from Farm to Market

> **We don't remove middlemen. We convert them into Verified Procurement Partners.**

AgriOS is an agricultural procurement platform connecting **companies, administrators, procurement partners, and farmers** through a structured workflow.

Instead of eliminating middlemen, AgriOS formalizes their role as **Verified Procurement Partners** with a fixed service commission of **₹2/kg**.

---

## 💡 How It Works

```text
Company
   ↓
Places Order
   ↓
Admin
   ↓
Approves Order
   ↓
Procurement Partner
   ↓
Accepts Order
   ↓
Sources from Farmers
   ↓
Transport & Delivery
   ↓
Company
```

### Example

A company places an order for **5,000 kg of tomatoes**.

* Company creates the order
* Admin reviews and approves it
* A verified procurement partner accepts the order
* Partner sources the tomatoes from farmers
* Partner coordinates collection and delivery
* Partner earns **₹2/kg = ₹10,000** as a service commission

## 🖥️ Three Portals

### 🏢 Company Portal

* Create procurement orders
* Specify produce and quantity
* Track order status

### 🛡️ Admin Portal

* View incoming orders
* Approve/reject orders
* Manage the procurement workflow

### 🚜 Procurement Partner Portal

* View approved orders
* Accept orders
* Manage procurement and fulfillment

---

## 🎯 Key Idea

Traditional middlemen already provide important services such as:

* Farmer relationships
* Produce aggregation
* Local procurement
* Collection and transportation

AgriOS doesn't try to remove them. Instead, it turns them into **verified, trackable procurement partners** with a clearly defined role and fixed commission.
